Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 6, 2026 · ZEROTOHIRE (AUTOAPPLY AI)

This Privacy Policy explains what data ZeroToHire ("the Service") collects, why, and how it's handled. The Service is an independently run project, and this policy is written plainly rather than as boilerplate.

1. What We Collect

DataWhy
Email, name, password hash Account creation and login (email/password, email OTP, or Google Sign-In)
Resume text, job descriptions, profile details (headline, projects, etc.) Sent to the AI pipeline to generate your resume, cover letter, ATS analysis, and interview prep
Job URLs you submit for fetching To pull job-posting text server-side for analysis
Application tracker entries, job-feed saves, autopilot preferences To power your dashboard, analytics, and the autopilot job-matching queue
Session cookie To keep you signed in between requests
One-time OTP codes (temporary) Passwordless sign-in via email, auto-expire after use

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your resume and job-description text are sent to Groq's API (running Meta's LLaMA 3.3 70B model) so the AI agents can generate your outputs. If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google profile info (name/email) for authentication only. If you use email OTP, we send codes via SMTP through our email provider.

We do not sell your personal data, resume content, or job-search activity to advertisers or third parties. We don't use your submitted content to train any AI model.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Account, profile, run history, job-feed, and tracker data is stored in a SQLite database on the server hosting the Service. Generated files (resumes, cover letters, exports) are stored per-run on the server and accessible only to your account. Data isn't shared across user accounts.

4. Cookies

The Service uses a session cookie strictly for authentication (keeping you logged in). It does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request full deletion of your account and associated data (profile, run history, tracker entries, job-feed saves) at any time by emailing malaichaap3@gmail.com. Deletion requests are typically processed within a few days.

6. Security

Passwords are stored using one-way hashing (never in plaintext). Input to every AI-facing endpoint is length-capped and type-validated to reduce abuse and crash risk. That said, no system is 100% secure, and this is an independently run project rather than an enterprise platform — please avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information (e.g. government ID numbers, financial account details) beyond what a normal resume or job application would contain.

7. Third-Party Processors

  • Groq — AI inference on resume/job-description text you submit
  • Google — optional OAuth sign-in
  • Email/SMTP provider — delivering OTP codes
  • Render (or equivalent hosting) — application hosting/infrastructure

Each of these providers processes only the minimum data needed for its function and is bound by its own privacy terms.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for job-seekers and students of working/internship age. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Service grows. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data-deletion requests, or anything else, reach out at malaichaap3@gmail.com.
